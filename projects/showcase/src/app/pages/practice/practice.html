Other modes

The grading round above only uses kana and latin. Everything else the field supports, outside any grading flow:

Tinted prompt card (sumi-ui#68)

appearance="tinted", once tone is set, is a stronger but still only tinted look — a 12% background tint, a 2px border all round and the app's sumi-pattern fading out behind the content, plus a chip filled solid in tone — never a fully coloured card. Per the chosen design (mockup variant D), the chip now names the item type; the question type (reading or meaning) is instead carried by the answer field below, via its own placeholder and modeCue.

Mode cue (sumi-ui#68)

modeCue makes the expected script visible on the field itself: a dark field with a あ tag for kana/katakana, a light field (in both themes) with an A tag for latin/romaji. A settled verdict still overrides background, border and text colour — including on the dark field, where white text on a pale verdict background would otherwise be unreadable.
